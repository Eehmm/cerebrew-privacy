Cerebrew Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Cerebrew ("the app") is a brain-games app for Android made by Henrik Rømer ("I", "me"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app. In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

Information the app stores

To work, the app stores the following on your device only:

  • Your settings: session length, focus areas, games you have turned off, difficulty mode, sound and vibration preferences.
  • Your game history: which games you played on which days, the difficulty level, your scores and how long each game took.
  • Today's planned session.

This information never leaves your device through the app. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send data anywhere. I have no access to it.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not ask for or collect your name, email address, contacts, location, photos, device identifiers, advertising ID or any other personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting services in the app.

Android backup

If you have turned on Android's backup feature, Android may include the app's settings and game history in your device backup to your Google account, so they can be restored on a new phone. Google handles that backup under Google's Privacy Policy. I have no access to it. You can turn off backup in your device settings.

Permissions

The app only uses the vibrate permission, for optional haptic feedback during games. You can turn it off with Settings → Vibrate on answers.

Deleting your data

You can erase your scores, levels and streaks at any time with Settings → Reset progress. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
